# SnapYaw

Snap the camera yaw to the nearest cardinal direction when looking close to it

---

Looking within 5 degrees of a cardinal direction (0/90/180/270) pulls the camera
the rest of the way toward it, reaching the exact angle in at most 3 seconds.
Turning back out of that 5-degree window stops the pull.

Pressing the snap key pulls the camera to the nearest cardinal from wherever
it's currently looking, reaching the exact angle in at most 1.2 seconds.

## Keybinds

| Action        | Bind     |
|---------------|----------|
| Toggle on/off | Numpad 1 |
| Snap now      | Numpad 2 |
